International transfers

The brief does not establish that an international transfer actually occurs, but it identifies a credible possibility for Airtable, Notion and the LLM provider. Before production processing, the client should establish the actual hosting/processing locations and whether any restricted transfer takes place.

If personal data is transferred from the EEA to a country without an applicable adequacy decision, an appropriate GDPR Chapter V transfer mechanism, such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), would generally be required, together with an assessment of the transfer circumstances and appropriate supplementary measures where necessary. No evidence that such a mechanism is currently in place is provided.
