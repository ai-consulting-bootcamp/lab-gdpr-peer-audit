# Phases 2–3 — Personal data and role map, clarifying questions log

**System:** Elif Yurdakul's Unioptt university programme fact-checker · **Auditor:** Pieter Albrecht · **Date:** 17 November 2026
**Scope:** the **production** design. The MVP uses synthetic profiles, so no real personal data is processed yet.

## CFU 1 — Recognize

- **Personal data categories:** student identity and contact (implied: Telegram account and chat ID; name not stated), grades, language test scores, budget (family finances), citizenship, age or minor status (implied), and the met/gap evaluation produced by the system. Logs in Airtable likely contain all of these.
- **Special-category data:** none collected by design. **Possible** if scholarship checks bring in disability or health data, and weakly inferable (ethnic origin) from citizenship plus name. Not Art. 9, but **children's data** and **financial data** both raise the risk level.
- **Crosses an EU border?** **Yes, likely:** Telegram (non-EU), Airtable (US), OpenAI (US, if the profile is in prompts). If Unioptt is Turkey-based, the whole flow may start outside the EU.
