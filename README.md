## Audit Scope

The audit covers:

* Personal data identification
* Personal-data and role mapping
* Controller / processor analysis
* Data Processing Agreements
* International transfers
* Lawful basis
* Purpose limitation
* Data minimisation
* DPIA requirement
* Automated decision-making and Article 22
* Transparency
* Data-subject rights
* Retention and deletion
* Third-party/vendor processing

The audit was conducted independently using the teammate's **Data Processing Brief** as the evidence base.

Where the brief did not provide enough information to reach a conclusion, the audit records **"Cannot determine from brief"** or **"TBD — legal review"** rather than making unsupported assumptions.

## Overall Recommendation

**Proceed with Conditions**

The independent audit identified material GDPR issues that should be resolved before the system processes live employee personal data, particularly:

* lawful basis for employee-related AI analysis;
* purpose limitation and employee transparency;
* DPIA requirement;
* international transfers;
* processor and DPA governance;
* retention and deletion;
* Article 22 / employment decision-making;
* potential sensitive or special-category data.

## Disclaimer

This repository is an educational GDPR peer-audit exercise.

The audit is **not**:

* a legal opinion;
* a formal DPIA;
* a GDPR certification;
* a substitute for advice from qualified legal counsel or a Data Protection Officer.
